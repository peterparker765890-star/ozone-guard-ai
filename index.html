03 / THE SCIENCE

Meet Earth's protective shield.

Understand where ozone exists, how depletion happens, and why international cooperation matters.

STRATOSPHERE OZONE-RICH REGION ATMOSPHERIC LAYERS
THE ESSENTIALS
01

What is the ozone layer?

A region of the stratosphere with relatively high ozone concentrations. It absorbs most incoming harmful UV-B radiation.

02

What causes depletion?

Certain human-made substances, including CFCs and halons, release chlorine or bromine in the stratosphere, which catalytically destroys ozone.

03

What is the Montreal Protocol?

An international agreement adopted in 1987 to phase out ozone-depleting substances. It has helped put the ozone layer on a path to recovery.

04

Is ozone depletion the same as global warming?

No. They are different problems, although some ozone-depleting substances are also powerful greenhouse gases.

05 / ECOPRODUCT INTELLIGENCE

Think beyond the label.

Different substances have different environmental properties. Learn the basics before making purchasing or servicing decisions.

❄️

CFC Refrigerants

Chlorofluorocarbons such as CFC-12 deplete stratospheric ozone and can have high global-warming potential. Their production and use have been heavily phased out under international controls, subject to applicable rules and exceptions.

OZONE-DEPLETING
🧪

HCFC Refrigerants

Hydrochlorofluorocarbons have lower ozone-depletion potential than many CFCs, but they still deplete ozone. They are also being phased out under international agreements.

TRANSITION SUBSTANCES
🌿

Alternative Refrigerants

Options such as HFOs, hydrocarbons, ammonia, and carbon dioxide have different climate, safety, efficiency, and equipment requirements. Some have low ozone-depletion potential, but not every alternative is suitable for every system.

EVALUATE EACH OPTION
Safety note: Refrigerants can be flammable, toxic, or operate under high pressure. Installation, recovery, and servicing should be carried out by appropriately trained professionals using compatible equipment.
